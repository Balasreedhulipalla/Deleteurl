Delete Account

If you would like to delete your account and all associated data from the Nano Meta-analysis Screening Tool, please follow the instructions below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  • Open the application
  • Go to your profile or settings section
  • Select the "Delete Account" option
  • Confirm your request

Data Deletion Details

Once your request is processed, all your personal data including login information and screening records will be permanently removed from our system.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 3–5 working days.
